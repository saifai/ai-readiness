What we propose: three parts, online, for each cohort

1Level 1 of the program

The program your students completed in person, now online for the graduating class.

Three steps

  1. Learn to build. A live online workshop: each student builds and publishes a web page with AI.
  2. Build a product. An app from their field, a career need or a business idea, guided by our team.
  3. Show it. An online Demo Day: students present their app as they would to an employer or a customer.

Two outcomes

Employability: a live app linked from their CV, and an interview-ready story about it.

Entrepreneurship: a working product, a method they can repeat, and the Keep Building Pack.

2AI Global Leaders Summit

Who speaks

Founders and AI technical leads of companies with multi-million-dollar annual revenue: technology companies, and ordinary businesses that built AI into how they work and saw major results.

Format

2days, online
3 hourseach day
3–4speakers per session
LiveQ&A

Each speaker covers what they built, its impact and the biggest opportunity for students, then takes questions.

Why it matters for KKU: students hear first-hand what employers and founders look for, from people with real track records.

3Monthly Support Sessions

What it is

Support after the program ends, so students are not left on their own: online, 90 minutes, once a month, for the 3 months after each cohort finishes.

What students bring

Practical questions from putting what they learned to use: a job interview they've had, a project they're working on or a business idea to test. They get direct, practical advice.

Why it matters for KKU

Support continues after delivery, and students become confident and capable with what they built.
