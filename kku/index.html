The program for your graduates

Three steps, delivered online

The same program your students completed in September and October, moved online so it can reach far more of them.

Learn to build

A live online workshop. Each student builds a web page with AI and publishes it.

Build their own app

Something from their field, their career or a real problem. Our team guides and supports them.

Show what they built

An online Demo Day, where students demonstrate their app to the judges.

You bring the students. We deliver the program.

What KKU provides

  • The students, college by college
  • Dates for each cohort
  • A coordinator in each college to confirm

What we provide

  • Instructors and the curriculum
  • Technical support while students build
  • Judging and an online Demo Day
  • A Keep Building Pack for every student

KKU doesn't need to write a new curriculum or hire its own AI instructors.

How it scales

From one cohort to the whole graduating class

KKU has around 15,000 graduating students. Sessions in a room can't reach that many. Online can.

Phase 1One online cohort

Run the program online with a first group of graduates. Size and dates to confirm

Phase 2College by college

Roll it out to each college in turn. Order of colleges to confirm

Phase 3The full graduating class

Every graduating student, around 15,000.

Students supporting students to confirm

Students from the first program help the next cohorts get started. Students listen to students, and these ones have done it themselves.

Every cohort ends with a showcase

Each group finishes with its own Demo Day, so KKU can see what every college built.
