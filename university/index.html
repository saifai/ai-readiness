How it works

Three steps, delivered online

The same program King Khalid University students completed in person, moved online so it can reach your whole graduating class.

Learn to build

A live online workshop. Each student builds a web page with AI and publishes it.

Build a product

An app from their field, a career need or a business idea. Our team guides and supports them.

Show it

An online Demo Day. Students present their app the way they would to an employer or a customer.

For large classes

Classes of several hundred or more run in waves, so each wave builds on the last. A class of 1,000, for example, runs as three cohorts of 250, 300 and 450.

Students work in groups of no more than 80. Each group has a facilitator and assistants, and an AI assistant answers routine questions.

Before day one

Every student is set up before the first session: tools installed, accounts ready, and a support group to ask when they are stuck.

The university nominates students and a coordinator. We run everything else.

No technical background needed.

Two levels

From a working app to real software

Most people building with AI stop at the first working version. Making it safe and reliable for real users is a separate skill, and it is the one organizations need. Graduates who can do both stand out.

Level 1 · Build

A working app, published live

Students learn to go from an idea to a working app with AI, publish it on the web, and present it. This is the program King Khalid University's students completed.

  • A live app linked from their CV
  • A method they can repeat for the next idea
  • A Demo Day presentation
Level 2 · Deploy

Software that real people can use

For students who complete Level 1. They take their app further, towards something an organization could rely on.

  • Secure logins and user permissions
  • Working with real data
  • Keeping AI costs under control as usage grows
  • Checking an app for security weaknesses
